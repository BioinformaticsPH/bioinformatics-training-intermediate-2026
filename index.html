Intermediate Plant Genome Bioinformatics Workshop

26–30 October 2026 | SEARCA Headquarters, UPLB Campus, College, Los Baños, Laguna

Hands-on, in-person plant genome bioinformatics training — command-line workflows, variant discovery, gene function analysis, and leveraging AI tools to accelerate data processing, troubleshooting, and trait discovery. Join us face-to-face and work through real plant genomic datasets alongside the instructors.
